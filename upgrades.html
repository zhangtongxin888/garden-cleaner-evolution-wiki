Upgrades

Build a smoother cleanup loop.

A practical decision framework for tools, new areas, quests, and rebirths—with no invented prices, tiers, or bonus values.

What is verified

The official description confirms tool upgrades, unlockable rewards and areas, quests and titles, secrets, and rebirth bonuses. Exact numbers and optimal builds are not currently verified.

The upgrade compass

Ask one question before spending or resetting: what is stopping the next satisfying cleanup? Your answer points to the most sensible category.

A

Cleaning feels slow

Review the tool upgrades currently shown in-game. Favor the improvement that directly addresses the task you repeat most.

B

The garden feels finished

Look for the game’s next area requirement, quest, reward, or prompt. Progress outward rather than over-investing in a completed space.

C

Objectives feel scattered

Return to quests and titles for direction. They are confirmed progression features and may make your next target clearer.

D

A rebirth is available

Compare the in-game stated bonus with everything the confirmation says will reset. Avoid a reset made only from impatience.

When to save instead

Saving can be the right choice when the current upgrade does not fix a real bottleneck, when a new area is close, or when the game’s current information is incomplete. You do not need to spend just because an option is visible.

Claims this guide does not make

  • No exact tool names or tier order without an official source.
  • No costs, multipliers, capacity values, or rebirth thresholds.
  • No “best build” based on an unavailable or changing experience.
  • No secret locations presented as confirmed.
