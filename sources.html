How claims are handled

Verified

Directly supported by an official source tied to the game, creator, or Roblox platform.

Time-stamped

Status information is paired with a check date because availability can change.

General guidance

Strategy that does not claim a specific hidden value or undocumented mechanic.

Not published

Rumored codes, exact stats, secret locations, or mechanics without adequate evidence.

Corrections

If an official page changes or you find a source-backed error, send the exact claim, the official link, and what should be corrected through our contact page. We prioritize traceable evidence over speed.
