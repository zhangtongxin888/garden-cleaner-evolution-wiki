What the game is about

Garden Cleaner Evolution is a Roblox experience about collecting leaves and restoring gardens. The official description frames the game as a cozy, low-stress ASMR cleaning experience, with a progression loop that grows beyond the first cleanup.

Confirmed activities

🍂

Collect leaves

Clear away debris as the foundation of the cleaning loop.

🌼

Restore gardens

Turn messy outdoor areas into cleaner, refreshed spaces.

🧰

Upgrade tools

Complete stated objectives and work toward titles.

Reset progression in exchange for bonuses, as officially described.
